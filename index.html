The shape of your year

2026 asked you to carry a lot. 2027 asks you to put some of it down.

Mila, 2027 is a reset year for you. A few rare cycles all start again within the same twelve months. That kind of timing only comes around a handful of times in a life.

  • A new emotional chapter starts in January. Your progressed Moon (the slow inner clock of your feelings) comes back to where it was the evening you were born. This happens about once every 27 years. It resets what home means and where you feel you belong.
  • A new 12-year cycle of confidence and luck starts in late February. Jupiter, the planet of growth, crosses your Leo rising sign.
  • Your life purpose resets in November. The Moon's nodes, the points in your chart that show direction and destiny, return to where they were when you were born.
  • The pattern that keeps you stuck between duty and self-sacrifice finally gets an outlet. The eclipse on 20 February lands right on it.

Read the rest with this in mind: the year supports change. The more of it you choose yourself, the gentler it feels.

Why 2026 felt so heavy

You were born with Jupiter and Pluto in an exact square, a drive to grow and transform that can also bring power struggles. In February 2026, Saturn and Neptune met at the very start of Aries, right on top of that square. Saturn brings pressure and Neptune dissolves things, so structures you relied on stopped holding. Then Saturn moved over your Venus and your Chiron (your oldest wound), and Uranus shook up the top of your chart, where career lives. It was a lot. That pressure eases in 2027.

The heart: Venus, Neptune and your path

You love with your whole soul, and your purpose is tied to that spiritual, devoted kind of love. Venus also sits next to Chiron and opposite Pluto, so love has come with intensity and old wounds.

Where to live

The same chart sits differently on different parts of the map.

When you move, your birth planets stay the same but your houses shift. In other words, which part of your life each planet lights up depends on where you live. This is called relocation astrology.

Your brightest planets have been sitting in your chart's back room. You give quietly until you are empty, and the world doesn't get to see you. That matches how 2026 felt.

Family and your parents

How do I leave them? The chart has an answer.

The guilt you feel about leaving your parents is in your birth chart. Saturn sits at the top of your chart, which is the house of parents, at the last degree of its sign. A last degree often marks something you're meant to complete and lay down, not carry for the rest of your life. Neptune in your home house is the pull to sacrifice yourself for family. Your Moon is caught between the two.

2027 is the year that pattern gets released:

  • 26 Dec 2026 and 23 Mar 2027: Uranus, the planet of sudden change, sets off the whole pattern. Something in the family set-up shifts, often in a way that frees you without you having to force it.
  • 20 February 2027: the lunar eclipse falls exactly opposite your Moon. It lands in the empty side of the pattern, the side that has never had an outlet. This is the moment for the honest family conversation.
  • 16 May 2027: Uranus makes an exact opposition to Neptune in your home house. The home itself changes.
  • Late April to October 2027: Neptune sits on your Venus, the planet that rules your parents' house. Family health or money news may feel foggy or emotional. Get clear facts and have the care plan agreed before April.
  • June to October 2027: Saturn supports your Sun. This is when a responsible plan for care, visits and help from others holds firm.

The way through

Your chart describes a base abroad with planned trips back. You don't have to choose between you and them. Saturn spends 2027 in your house of long journeys, so regular travel home becomes a steady structure in your life. A daughter who is burnt out and hidden can't give much. A daughter who is well, living her life and coming home regularly can give far more. If your parents stay in the US, Europe keeps you within an overnight flight of them, which Australia doesn't.

Your children: Jupiter, your planet of joy, sits in your house of children. Jupiter returns to make a lovely angle to it on 2 August, and the full Moon of 19 June lights it up. Expect happy moments and celebrations with your kids.

If you're single, these are the signatures of meeting someone significant. Either way, Europe puts Jupiter in your partnership house, and London sits right on your Jupiter line. Your love planets are hidden in New Jersey and come forward abroad.
